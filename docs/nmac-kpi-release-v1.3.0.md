# NMAC KPI — Release v1.3.0

## Release note (what users see)

**Headline:** New finance and scheduling KPIs, plus clearer revenue and lead labels

**Body:** Finance and revenue now includes **Net Income Margin**, **Revenue Trend vs. Prev Month**, and a renamed **Billed Revenue (Run-Rate)** chart (same data as before, clearer label). Scheduling adds **Appointment Confirmation Rate** and **Avg. Check-In → Check-Out**, and **Lead → Appointment Conversion** replaces the old lead conversion name. Enter monthly values for the new KPIs under Administration → NMAC master.

---

## Changelog

- **new** — Net Income Margin on Finance & revenue
- **new** — Revenue Trend vs. Prev Month on Finance & revenue
- **new** — Appointment Confirmation Rate on Scheduling
- **new** — Avg. Check-In → Check-Out on Scheduling
- **improved** — Renamed Monthly Revenue ($) to Billed Revenue (Run-Rate)
- **improved** — Renamed Lead Conversion Rate to Lead → Appointment Conversion
