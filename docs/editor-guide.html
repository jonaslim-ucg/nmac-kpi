NMAC KPI

Editor guide with screenshots

North Shore Medical Center · Bitrix24

Purpose & sign-in

NMAC KPI shows practice performance: this year vs last year vs goals. Open it from Bitrix24; you sign in with your Bitrix email. If you see Access not available, ask an admin to add you as Editor. Check your name and role at the bottom of the sidebar.

Each section below matches one screenshot. Follow the steps, then find the same labels in the photo.

FIGURE 1

View charts (read only)

Where in the app: Left sidebar → NMAC master KPI → e.g. Performance overview (other chart pages work the same way)

  1. Use these pages to review numbers — you do not enter data here.
  2. Change FY year and filters at the top right.
In this screenshot:
  • Left menu = chart sections (Performance overview, Visit volume, etc.)
  • Large chart = weekly comparison (this year, last year, target)
  • Top right = year selector (e.g. FY 2026)
FIGURE 2

Data entry — weekly KPIs

Where in the app: Left sidebar → Administration → Data entry

  1. Choose KPI and Year at the top.
  2. Enter this year and last year for each week.
  3. Click Add week if needed, then Save.
In this screenshot:
  • Highlighted Data entry in the sidebar (under Administration)
  • KPI and Year dropdowns at the top
  • Table rows = Week 1, Week 2, … with two number columns
  • Save button — click when finished
FIGURE 3

NMAC master — Targets (goals)

Where in the app: Administration → NMAC master → Targets tab (selected)

  1. Under Applies to, choose Full year or one month.
  2. Enter target numbers in the list.
  3. Click Save targets at the bottom.
In this screenshot:
  • Targets tab is active (not Monthly actuals)
  • Applies to dropdown = Full year or a single month
  • KPI list with target values + Save targets
FIGURE 4

NMAC master — Monthly actuals

Where in the app: Administration → NMAC master → Monthly actuals tab (selected)

  1. Click the Monthly actuals tab.
  2. Choose the month (month buttons/chips).
  3. Enter this year and last year for each KPI.
  4. Click Save month, then refresh Bitrix to update charts.
In this screenshot:
  • Monthly actuals tab is active (not Targets)
  • Title: “Monthly this year / last year”
  • Month selector and data grid with two columns per KPI
  • Save month button at the bottom of the form
FIGURE 5

Settings

Where in the app: Left sidebar → Administration → Settings

  1. Account: enter first/last name → Save name.
  2. Clear cache: if charts look wrong after saving → Clear cache → confirm → refresh Bitrix.
  3. Use Sign out on a shared computer.
In this screenshot:
  • Account card — your name and role
  • NMAC charts — Clear cache section with Clear cache button
  • Sign out at the bottom of Account

Good habits

  • Always Save / Save targets / Save month — nothing auto-saves.
  • One person per week or month when possible.
  • After big updates, open a chart (Figure 1) to verify numbers.
  • Charts still wrong? Use Figure 5 → Clear cache → refresh.

If something goes wrong

  • No access — admin adds your Bitrix email as Editor.
  • Missing Data entry or NMAC master — confirm Editor role.
  • Save failed — read the message and try again.
  • Wrong charts — Figure 5, Clear cache, refresh.
