NMAC Provider Experience Survey

Changed & added questions — structured and scaled format
Northshore Medical & Aesthetics Center · July 2026

Overview

Questions 5, 6, and 7 were converted from open-ended text fields into structured choices and 1–5 rating scales. This makes responses easier to analyze and report on. Questions 8–20 were renumbered but not changed in wording.

Questions removed (replaced)

Old Q5 (required, free text):
Who was your provider and what services did they treat you for (Annual Exam, Cardio Specialist, Weight loss, etc)?
Old Q6 (optional, free text):
How has your health, confidence, or quality of life improved since receiving care from NMAC?
Old Q7 (optional, free text):
What would you say to someone considering becoming a NMAC patient?

New & changed questions

5. What type of service did you receive? *

Type: Single choice (required)

  • Primary care / Annual exam
  • Cardiology
  • Dermatology / Aesthetics
  • Weight loss
  • Lab / Diagnostics
  • Rheumatology
  • Other (please specify)

6. How would you rate your provider? *

Type: 1–5 scale (required)

1 = poor · 5 = excellent

Care outcomes

7. Since receiving care at NMAC, how would you rate the improvement in your overall health? *

Type: 1–5 scale (required)

1 = no improvement · 5 = significant improvement

8. Since receiving care at NMAC, how would you rate the improvement in your confidence in managing your health? *

Type: 1–5 scale (required)

1 = no improvement · 5 = significant improvement

9. Since receiving care at NMAC, how would you rate the improvement in your overall quality of life? *

Type: 1–5 scale (required)

1 = no improvement · 5 = significant improvement

10. Would you like to share more about how your care has made a difference?

Type: Free text (optional)

Recommendation

11. How likely are you to recommend NMAC to a friend or family member? *

Type: 1–5 scale (required)

1 = not at all likely · 5 = extremely likely

12. Would you encourage someone considering NMAC to become a patient? *

Type: Yes / No (required)

13. What would you say to someone considering becoming a NMAC patient?

Type: Free text (optional)
