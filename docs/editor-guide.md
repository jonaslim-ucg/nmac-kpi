# NMAC KPI — Editor guide

A short guide for people who **enter and update numbers** in the dashboard. You do not need technical knowledge to use this.

---

## Getting in

1. Open **NMAC KPI** from **Bitrix24** (same place you open other workplace apps).
2. You are signed in automatically with your Bitrix email.
3. If you see **“Access not available”**, your email is not on the user list yet. Ask an **administrator** to add you with the **Editor** role.

At the bottom of the sidebar you’ll see your name, email, and role (**Editor**).

---

## What you can do (Editor)

| You can | You cannot |
|--------|------------|
| View all KPI charts and reports | Add or remove users |
| Enter **weekly** KPI numbers | Change organization-wide switches (sample data, hide menus) |
| Set **NMAC master** targets and monthly figures | — |
| Clear the **chart cache** if numbers look wrong | — |
| Update your **display name** in Settings | — |

**Viewers** can only look at charts. **Admins** can do everything editors can, plus manage users and organization settings.

---

## Sidebar — where to go

### NMAC master KPI (charts)

Read-only for you in terms of layout — use these pages to **check** performance:

- Performance overview, Visit volume, Scheduling, Finance, Calls, Nursing, Specialty, Compliance

Use the **year** and **filters** at the top to change what you see.

### Administration (your work)

- **Data entry** — weekly numbers (older “Practice” KPIs).
- **NMAC master** — yearly targets and **monthly** this year / last year numbers (feeds the main NMAC charts).
- **Settings** — your name and **reset chart cache** (see below).

If you don’t see **Data entry** or **NMAC master**, ask an admin to confirm your role is **Editor**.

---

## 1. Data entry (weekly KPIs)

Use this when you need to enter numbers **by week** (e.g. Week 1, Week 2…).

1. Go to **Administration → Data entry**.
2. Choose the **KPI** and **year** at the top.
3. Type **this year** and **last year** for each week.
4. Click **Add week** if you need a new week row.
5. Click **Save** when you’re done.

Wait for the green confirmation message. If something fails, try again or tell your admin.

---

## 2. NMAC master (targets and monthly data)

This is the main place for **NMAC dashboard** numbers.

Open **Administration → NMAC master**. There are two tabs:

### Targets

Goals for the fiscal year (and optional **per-month** overrides).

1. Stay on **Targets**.
2. Under **Applies to**, pick **Full year** or a specific month (e.g. January only).
3. Enter or adjust target numbers in the list.
4. Click **Save targets** at the bottom.

**Tip:** Full year is the default for every month. Use a single month only when that month’s goal should differ from the rest of the year.

### Monthly actuals

Real results for each calendar month.

1. Open the **Monthly actuals** tab.
2. Choose the **month** you’re updating.
3. Enter **this year** and **last year** values for each KPI.
4. Save that month (use the save control on that screen).

After saving, charts should reflect your numbers when people refresh the app.

---

## 3. Settings

Open **Administration → Settings**.

- **Account** — set your first and last name (shown in the sidebar), then **Save name**. Use **Sign out** when finished on a shared PC.
- **NMAC charts → Clear cache** — use this if charts show old or blank data after you’ve saved correct numbers. Click **Clear cache…**, confirm, then **refresh** the page or reopen the app in Bitrix.

You will **not** see organization toggles (sample data, hide menus). Those are for **admins** only.

---

## Good habits

- **Save** after each batch of changes; don’t leave the page assuming it auto-saved.
- **One person per area** where possible — avoid two editors saving the same week or month at the same time.
- After big updates, open a chart page and confirm the lines/numbers look right.
- If numbers still look wrong after saving, try **Clear cache** in Settings, then refresh.

---

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|--------|------------|
| Can’t open the app / “no access” | Ask admin to add your Bitrix email as **Editor** |
| Don’t see Data entry or NMAC master | Same — role must be Editor or Admin |
| Save failed or red error | Note the message, wait a moment, try again |
| Charts don’t match what you entered | Clear chart cache in Settings, refresh Bitrix tab |
| Wrong year or KPI on screen | Check the **year** and **KPI** dropdowns before entering data |

For account access or role changes, contact your **NMAC KPI administrator** (not IT unless they manage the app).
